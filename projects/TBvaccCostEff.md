## Project pitch

- Get a hand experience on implementing a simple TB model, with vaccine efficacy and coverage. Learn extraction.
- Overall aim to estimate the population-level health impact and cost effectiveness of an adult TB vaccine in South Africa.
- Modify model structure **SEIR**, the **E** state to incorporate vaccine and coverage. R code will be provided.
- Simple calibration approach to SA TB mortality data from Statistics South Africa.
  - Will provide 1997-2020 extract and show how it was cleaned, adjusted for completeness.
- Estimate impact of vaccine.
- _If there is time_: Estimate costs and cost-effectiveness

## Research area(s)

- Infectious disease dynamics
- Cost-effectiveness analyses
- Epidemiological
- Public health impact
- TB

## Data

### Type of data

Publicly available recorded deaths from the vital register.

### Source

### Description and status of data

- Literature based input costs:

  - treatment

Will use R.
